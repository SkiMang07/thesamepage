## Method

Sources: r/EngineeringManagers, r/managers, r/ExperiencedDevs, Hacker News, Ask a
Manager, TrustRadius. ~120 verbatim quotes collected.

The research passes were given the ICP and a one-line product description and nothing
else. They did **not** receive the H1, the taglines, the bar metaphor, the
never-say-"track" rule or the voice register.

Reddit is blocked by the Cowork session's egress policy. Quotes were retrieved through
the Arctic Shift archive API as stored comment and post bodies rather than rendered
pages. Nothing is paraphrased into quotation marks.

## Verified quotes

These four are load-bearing and were independently re-verified at source after the
research pass. All match character for character:

- "For each person I'm basically trying to reconstruct six months from half-memory."
- "Memory is not a system, I guess."
- "It always feels like I'm either slacking on it or building a creepy dossier on my own team."

Also verified, the originating post's own question: *"How do those of you with more
than a handful of reports actually do this? without it turning into surveillance?"*

Everything else should be re-verified at source before it goes into public copy.

## Contamination audit

The research passes were given only the ICP and a one-line product description. They
did not receive the H1, the taglines, the bar metaphor, the never-say-"track" rule or
the voice register. The language bank, the doc-per-person finding, the failure modes,
the AI line, the trigger moments and the entire "category says" column are therefore
clean.

Four claims in the first draft were contaminated by prior decisions and have been
removed:

| Claim | Correction |
|---|---|
| "The H1 stops being reopened" | Removed. A validated mechanism was collapsed into an unvalidated sentence. |
| "The research supports the bar metaphor" | Removed. The calibration gap is evidenced; "bar" as its name is not. |
| Our vocabulary in the "what they say" column | Split into verified and unverified tables. |

Counterfactual read: run blind, the audience layer comes out the same. What would
likely differ is which finding leads, which is the open question above.

**Layer 3 must be written cold**, before anyone reads a line of the current site.

## Sampling gaps — read before trusting a conclusion

- **No willingness-to-pay evidence at all.** Nothing supports or contradicts $20/mo
  from a manager's own pocket.
- **Engineering-heavy corpus.** Only three first-person GTM-manager accounts were
  found, and GTM managers are in the ICP. If one round of interviews gets funded, fund
  that one.
- **Ask a Manager is over-represented** and its vocabulary does not match the ICP
  ("my employee", "supervisor", "one-on-one" rather than "my team", "my reports", "1:1").
- **Review-site manager voice barely exists.** G2 and Capterra would not serve review
  bodies; the TrustRadius entries carrying a manager job title are one-line feature
  requests rather than real complaints.
- **Astroturf contamination** is heavy in the newer Reddit threads. The single best
  thread contains at least five founders plugging their own 1:1 tools.

## Next research, if funded

Interviews with 5-8 GTM managers matching the sharpened ICP, and any willingness-to-pay
signal at all. Both gaps are the kind only a conversation closes.
