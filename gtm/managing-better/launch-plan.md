# Managing Better — launch plan

## Inventory target

- **Launch day:** ten approved posts live.
- **Queue:** five additional approved posts, one at each of `L+7`, `L+14`, `L+21`,
  `L+28` and `L+35`.
- Replace `L` only when Andrew confirms the launch date and publication time zone.
- Every queued post is pushed and visually verified as a HubSpot draft before it is
  scheduled.

## Opening catalogue mix

Weight the catalogue toward review season without making the publication look like a
single campaign:

| Theme | Launch-day target | Queued target | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Review cycle | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| Feedback and hard conversations | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| Expectations and accountability | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| 1:1s and continuity | 2 | 0 | 2 |
| Remote management and trust | 1 | 1 | 2 |

This is a portfolio constraint, not a title list. Query research and Andrew's source
material decide the actual posts.

## Format balance

The first ten should demonstrate all four formats. A reasonable starting shape is
four Playbooks, two Teardowns, two Scripts and two Notes. Do not force a topic into a
format to hit the count; adjust the portfolio instead.

## Readiness board

Use one of these states in each post's publishing card:

`idea` → `briefed` → `researched` → `drafted` → `in review` → `approved` →
`HubSpot draft` → `verified` → `scheduled` or `published`

No post skips `approved`, `HubSpot draft` or `verified`.

## Launch-date resolution

When `L` is known:

1. Pick one consistent publication time in `America/New_York`.
2. Convert each slot to an explicit ISO 8601 timestamp including its UTC offset.
3. Check US daylight-saving changes across all five dates.
4. Review the order as a reader journey, not only as five independent keywords.
5. Authorize scheduling only after the remote draft for every slot is verified.
