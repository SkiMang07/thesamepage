# Managing Better — backlog

only after the search intent, Andrew's point of view and the evidence available for
it are clear enough to make a useful piece.

## Intake fields

When an idea is promoted, its brief records:

- working question and search intent;
- one format: Playbook, Teardown, Script or Note;
- primary messaging pillar and relevant trigger moment;
- Andrew's thesis and raw supporting material;
- sources already available and claims still needing research;
- relationship to existing or planned posts;
- intended launch slot, if any.
