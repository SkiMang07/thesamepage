Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and what we never do with it.

Effective August 25, 2026 · Last updated August 25, 2026

The Same Page (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates The Same Page, a team-management platform accessible at app.thesamepage.xyz. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect on behalf of your organization, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

1. What We Collect

Account Information

When you or your organization creates an account, we collect your email address, name, and a hashed password, or your authenticated email if you sign in with an identity provider.

Organization and Team Data

When someone sets up The Same Page for their team, we store the organization’s structure — its teams, reporting lines, and who leads what.

Performance and Development Data

This is the core of the product: goals, projects, priorities, capacity plans, meeting notes, role expectations, wrap-up summaries, and the rolling assessments a manager records about their team’s work. This is the record The Same Page exists to hold.

AI-Drafted Content

When you use an AI-assisted feature — insight generation, assessment drafting, or wrap-up extraction — we generate draft content from the data above using Anthropic’s Claude models. Drafts are always shown to you for review before anything is saved; nothing AI-generated becomes part of the record without a person confirming it.

Usage Data

We collect basic server logs (request timestamps, response codes) for reliability and security. We do not use analytics SDKs or ad-tracking pixels.

2. Data About Other People on Your Team

Unlike a typical single-user app, The Same Page is built to hold information about other people — specifically, the people you manage. If you’re a manager, your account may include your direct reports’ names, roles, goals, meeting notes, and assessment history, entered by you as part of normal use.

Your organization is responsible for having the right to collect and store this information about its employees, and for telling its employees how that information is handled internally — that’s a matter between your organization and its people, not something this policy can settle on your organization’s behalf.

Some team members may also have their own login to The Same Page — for example, to see their own goals or complete a check-in. Where that’s the case, this policy covers their account data the same way it covers yours.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and operate The Same Page for your organization
  • Generate AI-drafted content — insights, assessment drafts, meeting extraction — always shown to you for review before anything is saved
  • Maintain accounts and authenticate identity
  • Respond to support requests

We do not:

  • Sell your data or your team’s data to any third party
  • Display advertising of any kind
  • Share your organization’s data with other organizations, recruiters, or anyone outside your own account

5. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services infrastructure. Row-Level Security is enforced at the database layer — your organization’s data is isolated from every other organization’s, and what any individual account can see within your organization is scoped by that database-level policy, not just by application code.

All data in transit is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS.

For more detail, see our Security Statement.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account or organization is active. If your organization deletes its account, its goals, projects, notes, and assessment records are permanently deleted from our database.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data (it is fully visible within the app)
  • Export your data (contact us to request a full export)
  • Delete your account and associated data, subject to your organization’s own administrative controls

If your information was entered by your manager as part of your organization’s account, start with your employer for requests about that data — your organization controls its own account, and we’ll support it in fulfilling your request.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Same Page is a workplace product intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by updating the “Last Updated” date above and, where appropriate, by email. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
