Why I built it

I’ve been managing people for over a decade: teams of three and teams of 23, managers of other managers, people in their very first job and people who’d been in their field for more than 20 years. I’ve been obsessed with getting better at it the whole way, and I’ve tried more systems than I’d like to admit.

I lean on a good, consistent system and on data for most of my decisions. The part of managing I love most is helping people learn, get better at their day-to-day and grow in their careers.

The Same Page pulls together a handful of tools I’d been cobbling together for myself over the years. My hope is that it becomes more than a piece of software: a way of managing that helps a lot of people be better at this than I was when I started.

It opens on 1 December. If you’d like to be one of the first 20 managers, or you think I’ve got something wrong, send me a note.
