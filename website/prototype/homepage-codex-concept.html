The system

Three moves. One clear view.

No new management theory to learn. The Same Page turns the work you already do into a record you can actually use.

Define what good looks like01

Write the bar once.

Metrics, skills and values, per role and level. The thing nobody writes down, written down once — and shared with the person expected to clear it.

Capture evidence as you go02

Work normally.

1:1s, check-ins, notes and commitments land against the person and the expectation. The record builds itself instead of being reconstructed.

See where everyone stands03

Open Monday to one thing.

One surface. Zoom out for the team, click in for the person, and know the facts behind the rating — plus what to say next.

Why nothing else does this

Manage with evidence, not instinct.
