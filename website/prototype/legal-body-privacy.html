The Same Page (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates The Same Page, a team-management platform accessible at app.thesamepage.xyz. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, who helps us run the service, and your rights regarding your data.

The short version: what you write in The Same Page belongs to you and your organization. We never sell it, never show ads, and never use it to train AI models. We measure how the app is used with counts and page views, never with what you typed.

What We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and name. Sign-in works by a link emailed to you, handled by our authentication provider, so we do not store a password.

Organization and Team Data

When someone sets up The Same Page for their team, we store the organization’s structure: its teams, reporting lines, roles, and who leads what.

Performance and Development Data

This is the core of the product: goals, projects, priorities, capacity plans, 1:1 and meeting notes, prep sheets, commitments, role expectations, wrap-up summaries, development plans, and the assessments a manager records about their team’s work. This is the record The Same Page exists to hold.

Files and Dictation

You can attach files (such as PDFs, Word documents, or meeting transcripts) and dictate notes by voice. Attached files and the text read from them are stored with your organization’s data. Dictated audio is sent for transcription and is not stored by us; only the resulting text you choose to keep is saved.

Call Transcripts

If you transcribe a 1:1, audio from your computer’s microphone (and, if you add it, the call’s browser tab) is sent to OpenAI in short pieces to be turned into text. We never store the audio. Neither does OpenAI: audio sent for transcription is not kept for abuse monitoring and is not used to train models. Only the text transcript is saved, labelled as a machine transcript. Lines are labelled by which source heard them, your microphone or the call tab, never by recognising anyone’s voice. Anything said while transcription is paused or off the record is not transcribed.

A transcript is evidence for your wrap-up, not part of the record by itself. Nothing from it enters your notes, summaries, or commitments until you review and save the wrap-up.

AI-Drafted Content

When you use an AI-assisted feature, such as a prep sheet, an assessment draft, or a meeting wrap-up, we generate draft content from the data above using third-party AI models. Drafts are always shown to you for review. Nothing AI-generated becomes part of the record until a person confirms it.

Usage and Diagnostic Data

We record how the app is used so we can improve it: which pages are visited and counts of actions (for example, that a prep sheet was saved, or how many people were added to a team). These records are tied to your account ID. They never contain what you typed: no notes, names, prompts, transcripts, or file contents. We discard IP addresses, and we do not use session recording, heatmaps, or ad-tracking pixels.

When something breaks, we capture a technical error report (what failed, where, and on which account) so we can fix it. We also keep basic server logs (timestamps, response codes) for reliability and security.

Data About Other People on Your Team

Unlike a typical single-user app, The Same Page is built to hold information about other people, specifically the people you manage. If you’re a manager, your account may include your direct reports’ names, roles, goals, meeting notes, and assessment history, entered by you as part of normal use.

Your organization is responsible for having the right to collect and store this information about its employees, and for telling its employees how that information is handled internally. That’s a matter between your organization and its people, not something this policy can settle on your organization’s behalf.

Before you transcribe a 1:1 with someone, you confirm they have agreed. That confirmation is stored with the date, and you can clear it from their page at any time. If they have their own login, they can read the transcript of their 1:1 once the call has ended.

Some team members may also have their own login to The Same Page, for example to see their own goals. Where that’s the case, this policy covers their account data the same way it covers yours.

How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide and operate The Same Page for you and your organization
  • Generate AI-drafted content, always shown to you for review before anything is saved
  • Maintain accounts and authenticate sign-in
  • Send service emails, such as sign-in links and reminders about your own meetings
  • Understand, in aggregate, how the product is used, and find and fix errors
  • Respond to support requests

We do not:

  • Sell your data or your team’s data to anyone
  • Display advertising of any kind
  • Share your organization’s data with other organizations, recruiters, or data brokers
  • Use your content to train AI models, ours or anyone else’s. Your content is sent to an AI provider only at the moment you use an AI feature, under commercial terms that do not permit the provider to train on it.

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Supabase on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Row-Level Security is enforced at the database layer: your organization’s data is isolated from every other organization’s, and what any individual account can see within your organization is scoped by that database-level policy, not just by application code. All data in transit is encrypted over HTTPS/TLS.

For more detail, see our Security Statement.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Deletion is permanent: it removes your account, your organization’s records, and your attached files from our database, and removes your contact and usage records from HubSpot and PostHog. We cannot recover an account once it has been deleted.

Call transcripts are kept until you delete them; there is no automatic expiry. You can delete a transcript at any time, on any plan, including a read-only account. Deleting it removes the text permanently. A log you already saved from it stays, because it is your own record.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access your data. It is all visible within the app.
  • Export your data. Settings lets you download everything your account holds as a single file.
  • Correct your data. Anything you entered can be edited in the app.
  • Delete your account and its data, as described above.

If your information was entered by your manager as part of your organization’s account, start with your employer for requests about that data. Your organization controls its own account, and we will help it fulfill your request. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law; contact us and we will respond.

Children’s Privacy

The Same Page is a workplace product intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
