A bit about me

I've managed people for more than 15 years: teams of all shapes and sizes, other managers, people in their first job and people who'd been working for over 20 years. Most of that has been in Customer Success.

For a long time my system was a Google Doc per person. Once the team got past seven or so, it stopped keeping up. I clicked through every doc before each 1:1, and honestly, the people who were steady and doing well were the ones whose notes I let slide. The Same Page is the tool I wish I'd had then.

I'm always happy to talk shop about managing people, customer success, or building with AI. If you're a manager and want to try it early, even better.
